What Actually is OpLinked?

A lightweight web-based intercom powered securely by Google Apps Script.

OpLinked runs smoothly on a powerful Google Apps Script backend. When you sign up, you are given a unique, private link dedicated entirely to your practice.

Because it lives on the web, there is nothing to download from an app store. You simply open the link on any computer, tablet, or smartphone browser.

For instant access, you can easily bookmark the link or pin it directly to your device's home screen, making it behave just like a native app right on your operatory walls or front desk screens.

💡 Setup in 3 Easy Steps:

  • 1 Get your unique practice link from OpLinked.
  • 2 Open it on any browser (iPads, tablets, PCs).
  • 3 Bookmark or add to home screen for 1-tap access!
